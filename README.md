# Wisetric Merchants

A modern business website for **Wisetric Merchants**, providing electrical engineering, lighting, renovation, consultation, event lighting, and material supply services.

## Dashboard Preview

![Wisetric Merchants Dashboard](./public/images/dashboard.png)

## Features

- Responsive and modern landing page
- Electrical and lighting services showcase
- Renovation and supply services
- Consultation and event lighting services
- Company information and portfolio sections
- Partners section
- Contact and quote request call-to-actions
- Clean dark navy and orange visual theme

## Sections

- **Home** – Introduction and key services
- **Services** – Electrical, lighting, renovation, and supply services
- **About** – Company information
- **Our Work** – Previous projects and work
- **Partners** – Business partners
- **Contact** – Contact information and inquiries
- **Portfolio** – Project showcase
- **Get a Quote** – Quote request call-to-action
